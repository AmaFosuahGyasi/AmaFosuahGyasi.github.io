About Me

I am a CELTA-certified native English teacher with several years of classroom experience in Ecuador. I help every student reach their full potential by using teaching strategies that fit their needs.

I have taught English, English Literature, science and social studies from kindergarten through high school, and I teach ESL online to young adults and professionals. I currently teach English Literature to 9th graders in Quito.

This fall I began a dual Master's degree at EIU, combining an MS in Technology with an MBA. It builds on my background in computer science and information systems and my earlier work in sales channel development at Nestlé Ghana. I'm aiming to bring together education, technology and business, and to apply that mix in learning and training environments.

I speak English (native), Twi (proficient) and Spanish (beginner). My first degree is registered with SENESCYT in Ecuador's national higher education system (SNIESE).
